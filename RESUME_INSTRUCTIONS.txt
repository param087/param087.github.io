📄 RESUME PDF INSTRUCTIONS
==========================

To enable the resume download functionality on your portfolio:

1. Create or export your resume as a PDF file
2. Name the file: resume.pdf
3. Place it in the root directory of this project (same folder as index.html)

Your file structure should look like:
Porfolio/
├── index.html
├── resume.pdf  <-- Add your resume here
├── README.md
└── assets/
    └── js/
        └── main.js

Alternative Approach:
---------------------
If you want to use a different filename (e.g., "Param_Bhavsar_Resume.pdf"):

1. Save your resume with your preferred name
2. Update the link in index.html:
   
   Find:
   <a href="resume.pdf" download>
   
   Change to:
   <a href="Param_Bhavsar_Resume.pdf" download>

Tips for Creating Your Resume:
-------------------------------
- Keep it to 1-2 pages
- Use a professional template
- Include your contact information
- Highlight your 4+ years of experience
- List key skills and technologies
- Include notable projects and achievements
- Proofread carefully

Online Resume Tools:
--------------------
- Canva (canva.com)
- Resume.io (resume.io)
- Zety (zety.com)
- Google Docs (with templates)
- Microsoft Word (with templates)

Once you add your resume.pdf file, you can delete this instruction file.
